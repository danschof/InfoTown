Using InfoTown

Take it. Teach with it. Make it yours.

InfoTown is free. Share it, print it, adapt it and use it to help people understand how information works. All we ask is a little credit, and that nobody sells it.

You can

  • Share the lessons and stories, and link to them
  • Print them, or show them in a training session or a classroom
  • Use them for your own organisation's in-house training
  • Adapt them: crop a comic panel into your slides, translate a story, or rewrite one for your own job

We ask that you

  • Credit InfoTown and link to infotown.co.uk
  • Don't sell it, or charge for a course built on it
  • Share your version the same way. If you adapt InfoTown and share the result, use this same licence, so it stays free for the next person
