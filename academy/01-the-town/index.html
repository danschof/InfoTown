The InfoTown term

Every organisation has a hidden town. Most people work there every day. They just haven't seen it yet.

The reveal

Looking after this town is Information Management!

Information management means planning, building and running the town, so the right information reaches the right people at the right time.

Standards such as ISO 19650 are rulebooks for running parts of the town on construction and infrastructure projects. InfoTown shows you the whole town first, so the rules make sense when you meet them in Lesson 10.
