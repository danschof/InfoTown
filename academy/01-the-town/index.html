The scene

Your hidden town

Behind the offices and desks you can see, your organisation has a hidden town: the digital world where its work really happens. Each district is a part of the business, with its own buildings (systems) and the people who go there to work. The town was here before your current project began, and it will still be here when the project ends. Switch on the transport network to see how parcels travel between districts.

The InfoTown idea

Every organisation has a hidden town. Most people work there every day. They just haven't seen it yet.

The reveal

Looking after this town is Information Management!

Information management means planning, building and running the town, so the right information reaches the right people at the right time.

Standards such as ISO 19650 are rulebooks for running parts of the town on construction and infrastructure projects. InfoTown shows you the whole town first, so the rules make sense when you see them all together in Lesson 12.
