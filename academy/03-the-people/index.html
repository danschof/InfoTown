The People

Who's doing what?

So far: we've seen the town (Lesson 1) and its buildings (Lesson 2). Now meet the people who fill them.

The scene

The Town Square on a busy morning

Everyone in the square does one of three things with information: they make it, check it or use it. Pick a job to see who's doing it. Most people turn up in all three.

The people test

For every kind of parcel, can you name…

Who makes it?

Written downA named role, so the work has an owner.
Not written downWhoever happened to pick it up.

Who checks it?

Written downA named checker who knows it's their job.
Not written downEveryone thinks someone else has.

Who says yes?

Written downOne person with the authority to approve it.
Not written downWhoever was last to see it, by default.
The real town

Why is it so hard to say who does what?

Because the people in the town don't stand still.

People wear many hats

The same person makes one parcel and checks the next.

Roles change mid-job

People join, leave and move around.

Titles don't say it

"Senior engineer" doesn't tell you what they check.

The org chart isn't the information chart

Who you report to isn't who checks your work.

A note for the town planners (that's IM): write down who makes, checks and says yes for each kind of parcel. One page is enough, as long as everyone can find it.

Not sure who?
Find out first.

  1. Ask who checks it before you start, not after you finish.
  2. Write the answer down.
  3. Put it where the next new starter will find it.
The InfoTown term

The town is full of people. Everyone makes, checks or uses information, and most people do all three.

The reveal

These are Appointing Parties, Appointed Parties and Task Teams!

ISO 19650 gives the groups of people in the town names, so everyone knows who works for whom.

The client is the appointing party. The organisation leading the work for them is the lead appointed party. The teams doing the work are task teams. Who makes, checks and approves each piece of information is written down in a responsibility matrix: your who-does-what list.
