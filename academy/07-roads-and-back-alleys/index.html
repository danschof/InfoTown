The scene

Every town has two ways across it

The proper route is the one we built. It goes through the Site Office, the project's CDE, where every parcel is booked in. The back alley is the shortcut people take when they're in a hurry. Both get the parcel there.

Show:
The proper route. Every stop is recorded.
Back alley. Faster, but it leaves no trail.
The InfoTown idea

Every town has routes we built and back alleys people use. Both move parcels. Only one leaves a trail.

The reveal

This is an Information Exchange!

The back alley is an informal exchange, and informal exchanges are a risk.

ISO 19650 expects information to be shared through the CDE, so every container carries its status and revision and leaves a record of who shared it. That's the proper route. Anything that goes round it skips those protections.
