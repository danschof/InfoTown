The reveal

This is your ISO 19650 process in action!

Plan it, set it up, make it, label it, share it, check it, hand it over, use it. That's the standard, in town language.

ISO 19650 is the international standard for managing information over the whole life of a built asset. ISO 19650-2 covers delivery: understanding what's needed, appointing the teams, mobilising, producing information together, and delivering it for authorisation and acceptance. The standard, your BEP and your procedures are the rules that make the whole town work.
