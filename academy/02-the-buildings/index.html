The building test

Is your work in a proper building?

Would it survive if your laptop died?

Proper buildingYes. It lives on the CDE, not on your machine.
The shedNo. It goes with the laptop.

Can your team find it without asking you?

Proper buildingYes. It's where everyone expects it.

Living in the shed?
Move in properly.

  1. Move your work in progress into your team's area on the CDE.
  2. Delete the shed copy so there's only one.
  3. Bookmark the right building so it's the quickest one to reach.
The InfoTown term

Information lives in buildings. Some are built to last, some are built for one project, and some are sheds nobody else can see into.

The reveal

This is a Common Data Environment!

The Post Office is the CDE: the agreed place where project information is kept, shared and looked after. The other buildings are the systems around it.

ISO 19650 describes the CDE as the agreed source of information for a project or asset, used to collect, manage and share every information container through a managed process. The other buildings still matter, but the CDE is the one everybody agrees to trust.
