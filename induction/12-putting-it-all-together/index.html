The scene

Drawing 042's whole journey

One line, twelve stops. Above each stop is the town word; below it is what the standard calls it. Every stop makes the next one possible, and skipping one shows up later, usually on site, usually on a Monday. Pick any stop to go back to that lesson, or follow the whole process, from the client's requirement to handover, and watch the labels change as it goes.

This is your ISO 19650 process in action!

Plan it, set it up, make it, label it, share it, check it, hand it over, use it. That's the standard, in town language.

ISO 19650 is the international standard for managing information over the whole life of a built asset. ISO 19650-2 covers delivery: understanding what's needed, appointing the teams, mobilising, producing information together, and delivering it for authorisation and acceptance. The standard sets the process, your BEP is the promise, and your rulebook says how. Together they make the whole town work.

Where to start
on Monday

  1. Draw your town: the buildings, the people, the routes and the back alleys.
  2. Pick the one back alley that hurts most, and build a proper route for it.
  3. Teach the town language: parcels, labels, checkpoints. The ISO words will follow.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): make sure you draw out the town before you try to fix it! You can't improve routes nobody can see...
