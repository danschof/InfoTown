Sam's Heads Up Check the pack is actually attached

InfoTown good practice: Every firm gets the same information requirements with the enquiry.

On your job: Your procurement system may send packs out automatically. Check the information requirements are attached, not just mentioned.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me the information requirements that went out with this enquiry.
  • How did you compare the tenders on information?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • The enquiry pack, with the information requirements in it.
  • A tender comparison that includes each firm's information response.

Where Rex comes in: Rex's Lesson 3, The People: the firms around the lead, each with its own job. Sam's Tender 8 is what happens to the pack when the contract is signed.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Passing the requirements down

ISO 19650 expects the lead firm to pass its information requirements to every firm it appoints, starting at tender, so each can say how it will meet them.

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI.
