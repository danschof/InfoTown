Watch out for

This is Asset Information!

Sam's Heads Up Get the actual template

InfoTown good practice: Product data comes back in the client's required format.

On your job: That might be a spreadsheet, a database export or a data schema. Get the real template at order stage, not a description of it.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me the product data requirements in this order.
  • How was the supplier's data checked before handover?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Orders that include the asset data requirements.
  • Supplier data in the client's format, checked before handover.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Asset information

ISO 19650 expects the asset information the owner needs to be set out in their requirements and delivered in the agreed format. Passing those requirements to suppliers through the order is part of passing them down the chain.

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. You'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
