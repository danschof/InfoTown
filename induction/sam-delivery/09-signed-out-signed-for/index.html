Handover is a delivery, not a dump truck. Every parcel is signed out by us and signed for by them. Send the client asset data in our format instead of theirs, and it's the wrong colour wardrobe: it goes straight back.

The scene

From the shared table to the published shelf

Parcels on the shared table carry S codes: shared, and fit for something. To become published, they make one more trip. The firm leading the work authorises the delivery, the client accepts it, and the S becomes an A. If it isn't right, it comes back with comments. And some parcels never make the trip at all. From the standard
ISO 19650 Authorise, then accept

ISO 19650 expects information to be authorised by the lead firm before it goes to the client, then reviewed and accepted by the client. Rejected? It comes back with reasons. Fix it, authorise it again, and resubmit. The trail shows all three.

StepWho
Review against the requirementsUs
AuthoriseUs, recorded
Review and accept (or reject with reasons)The client, recorded
Fix and resubmit, if rejectedUs, then them again
