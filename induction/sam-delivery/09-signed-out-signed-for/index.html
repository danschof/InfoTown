The right way

Before it goes

It's reviewed against what the client asked for, and authorised by us. The authorisation is recorded.

The client

The client accepts it, or rejects it with reasons. Rejected? Fix it, authorise it again, and resubmit. All recorded.

Handover

Only what's needed goes: the delivery list, not the drive.

The week after

Handover is a short list with everything ticked off. The payment arrives.

Where Rex comes in: Lesson 10: Handing Over

From the standard
ISO 19650 Authorise, then accept

ISO 19650 expects information to be authorised by the lead firm before it goes to the client, then reviewed and accepted by the client. Rejected? It comes back with reasons. Fix it, authorise it again, and resubmit. The trail shows all three.

StepWho
Review against the requirementsUs
AuthoriseUs, recorded
Review and accept (or reject with reasons)The client, recorded
Fix and resubmit, if rejectedUs, then them again

From the ISO 19650 series. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. InfoTown explains the ideas in its own words, for learning; you'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
