The right way, all day

8am

The assessor arrives. Helen opens the day, then leaves them to it.

9am

The project team is in the room: the project director, Stevie, Bev, Priya and Rex. Not just Sam.

10am

Assessor: Show me how you found the client's requirements.

Sam opens the requirements list. Tom explains the assumptions.

11am

Assessor: Show me who checked this drawing.

Rex finds the check record in the CDE in thirty seconds.

3pm

Two minor findings. Each one gets an owner and a date before the assessor has packed up.

4pm

Sam: We passed.

Sam rings Helen. Helen stares at her phone, deeply suspicious.
