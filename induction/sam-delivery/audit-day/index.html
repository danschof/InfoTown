Think like Sam

Audit day is a normal day where someone asks to see things. Pick one "show me" question a week and answer it yourself. If you have to go looking, that's your finding.

The right way, explained

The right way, explained

An inspector doesn't ask how the town's post works. They pick one parcel and follow it: who made it, who checked it, who signed for it, and where it lives now. If every stop left a stamp on the label, the walk takes ten minutes. If it didn't, it takes all day, and ends in somebody's inbox.

If you're the project manager

If you're the project manager

It's your audit too. Be in the room, bring the team, and make finding the evidence part of every week, not the week before.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Being audited

ISO 19650 expects the information process to be planned, carried out and recorded, so you can show it happened. An audit tests that by sampling: the assessor picks real items and asks to see the trail. Anything that falls short becomes a finding, and each finding needs an action, an owner and a date.
