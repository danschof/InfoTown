Where Rex comes in: Lesson 9: The Checkpoints

From the standard
ISO 19650 Checking and sharing

ISO 19650 expects every piece of information to be quality-checked against the project's standard, then reviewed and approved before it's shared, with a status that says what it can be used for.

Before sharingIn plain words
Quality checkMade to the rules? Named, labelled and formatted properly?
ReviewDoes it do the job it's for?
Approve for sharingSomeone who didn't make it says yes
StatusSays what it's fit for, and only that

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. You'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima.
