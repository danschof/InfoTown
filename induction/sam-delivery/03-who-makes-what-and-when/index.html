"What are we building?"

Every project starts with the client's question. The design team turns it into a list of drawings to make. The site team turns it into details, questions and inspections. Only then does anybody start making things, inside the right building.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Delivery plans and who does what

ISO 19650 expects each team to plan what information it will produce and when, and the lead firm to roll those into one master plan, alongside a detailed matrix of who is responsible for what.

ExpectIn plain words
Team delivery plansEach team's list: what, when, who
The master delivery planAll of them together, lined up with the programme
The responsibility matrixWho makes, checks and approves each piece
Kept liveUpdated whenever the programme or the scope moves

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI.
