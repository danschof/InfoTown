From the standard
ISO 19650 Reviewing the whole model

Before information goes for sign-off at a milestone, ISO 19650 expects the delivery team to review the whole information model together, not just each piece on its own.

The reviewIn plain words
WhenBefore every milestone, booked in the plan
WhatEveryone's work, together
Clashes and gapsEach one owned: fixed, or recorded honestly
What goesA package that fits, and a note of what's still open

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI.
