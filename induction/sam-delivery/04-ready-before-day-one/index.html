The right way, explained

The right way, explained

You don't open a town's roads until the signs are up and the drivers have passed their test. And not every driver gets a key to every building.

If you're the project manager Don't let anyone start without the training and the right access.

Don't let anyone start without the training and the right access. "They'll pick it up" costs more than a two-hour session.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me the training records and the test results.
  • Who can see what, and when was that last checked?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Training attendance.
  • Test records.
  • An access register, reviewed regularly.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Mobilising

ISO 19650 expects the team to mobilise before production: people trained, IT set up and working, and the ways of working tested, so the first real drawing isn't the test. ISO 19650-5 adds that access to sensitive information is on a need-to-know basis.

MobiliseIn plain words
PeopleTrained before they get a login
ITSet up, connected and tested
MethodsTried on a dummy, not on live work
AccessNeed-to-know, reviewed, and removed when people leave

From the ISO 19650 series. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. InfoTown explains the ideas in its own words, for learning; you'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
