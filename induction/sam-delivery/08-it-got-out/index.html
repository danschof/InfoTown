In the town See it in the town: the alarm's going off at the town gate. What happens first?

Sam's view: the alarm at the town gate. Stop it, work out what's gone, tell the client. In that order, and fast.

  1. 1 The alarm at the town gate: drawings of something that serves 40,000 homes have left the building. A pass is missing from the board.
  2. 2 What happens next is written down before it's needed: stop it, work out what's gone, tell the client the same day, then fix whatever let it out.
  3. 3 Everyone on site knows who to tell, because it was in their induction.

  • A security plan with an incident procedure.
  • Induction records that cover it.
  • An incident log with actions and lessons.

From the standard
ISO 19650 When information gets out

ISO 19650-5 expects a security-minded approach on sensitive assets, including a plan for what happens when something goes wrong: how it's reported, how it's contained, and who's told.

The plan saysIn plain words
What's sensitiveSo people know before they share
Who to tellA name, not "IT"
How fastHours, not days
What happens nextContain it, assess it, tell the client, learn from it

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. You'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima.
