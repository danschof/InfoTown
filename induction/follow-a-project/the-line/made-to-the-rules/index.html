The label
The details on the outside of every piece of work, so nobody has to open it: its name, its status and its revision.
Status codes (S4, S5)
Part of the label: what the work can be used for. S0 means it's still being made.
Revisions (P and C)
Part of the label: which version it is. P versions are drafts. C versions are published.
The rulebook

Every firm works to the project's rules: naming, templates, coordinates, how much detail each thing needs.
