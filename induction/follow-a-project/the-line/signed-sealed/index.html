The back alley's bill so far: 42 weeks · See the bill

The information

They've won. The promise goes into the deal with the neighbours now. I'm a promise with a signature on it.

The wrong way

Award…
We won

The client picks the firm. There's cake. Everyone goes to the cake.

The signature

Helen signs the contract. The information requirements are in Appendix K. Nobody opens Appendix K.

The subcontracts

The subcontracts go out without the client's order in them. The subbies sign up to drawings "by others".

Month four…
Appendix K

Helen: What did we sign?

Sam: Appendix K.

Helen: What's in Appendix K?

Sam: Everything.

The client asks why the asset data isn't being collected.

The cake was eaten in ten minutes. Appendix K took the rest of the job.

+2 weeks on the back alley's bill: Nobody read what was signed, and the subbies never got the order: two weeks re-papering every subcontract.

The right way

Award…
We won

There's cake. Sam looks for the contract before the cake.

Before signing

The promise, the who-does-what list and the parts list are confirmed with the client and become part of the contract between the two firms.

The signature

Helen: So we're promising the asset data in their numbers. Good. I'd like to know that before I sign it.

Helen signs. She knows what the information promises are, because somebody showed her first.

Every subcontract

The client's order goes into every subcontract too, so every firm on the job has signed up to its part.

Month four…
No surprises

The asset data is being collected, because everyone signed up to collecting it. Appendix K is dull, which is how it should be.

Now there is: The deal with the neighbours (the appointment), with the promise inside it. The order in every subcontract. The who-does-what list and the parts list, agreed.

What's going on here

Once the contract is signed, the promise is a contract term. Putting the promise, the who-does-what list and the parts list inside it, and the order inside every subcontract, is how the whole team ends up bound to the same information.
