Now there is: Orders and subcontract work from the published shelf (published information, current version).

What's going on here

An order is information leaving the job, so it carries the version it was placed against. Ordering from the published shelf, and giving every new firm its keys and a tour on day one, means everyone buys and builds from the same current version, not whatever was in their inbox or their office printer.

Keys on day one
Logins and access for a new firm, set up the day it starts.
In the town See it in the town: An inbox copy looks just as official. Does it know it's old?

The pump order is information leaving the job for a supplier, so it carries a revision with it. C02 had been on the published shelf in the Site Office for a fortnight, while C01 sat in Bev's inbox looking smart. New firms bring old copies too, like Nicole's March prints. Keys on day one give every new gang a way into the Site Office, so the shelf, not the inbox or the office printer, is where everyone buys and builds from.

Fix it Orders already out? Check which revision they quote.
  1. List this month's orders and the revision each was placed against.
  2. Compare each with the current revision on the published shelf.
  3. Give any firm still without logins its keys this week.

A note for the information manager: Make the revision number a required field on every order form, so "which spec?" gets answered before the lorry arrives.

Try it on your job

Pick one order placed on your job this month. Does it say which version of the specification it was placed against?
