The right way

Week one of the dig…
The cabin wall

The method statement, C01, is briefed and pinned to the cabin wall. The briefing sheet says which version: C01.

The old signs come down

When C02 goes on the published shelf, Priya, who looks after quality and safety, re-briefs the gang from it and takes C01 off the wall herself.

Show me your working

Rex's crane pad calculation is checked by someone who looks at the numbers going in, not just the sums. The pump weight comes from the current schedule.

Lift day…
The lift

The lift goes ahead on a checked calculation and a current method statement. The pump goes in. The crane goes home on time.

Now there is: Briefings from the current version, with the old signs down (document control). A checked calculation behind the permit.

What's going on here

A briefing passes information from the page to the people doing the work, so it has to be the current page. Writing the version on the briefing sheet, re-briefing when it changes and taking the old copy down keeps the gang and the drawing in step. A check that looks at what went into a calculation, not just the sums, is what makes the tick mean something.

New at this stop
