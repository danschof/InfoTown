Sam writes the plan for day one: who sets up the Site Office, who gets logins, who's trained, by when, and what has to be tested before anyone uses it.

It's priced into the bid, including the time to test with dummy files before anyone needs it.

What's going on here

The first weeks of a job decide where information will live for the rest of it. Planning day one during the bid, with names, dates and a price, means the Site Office is ready before anyone needs it.
