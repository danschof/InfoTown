The back alley's bill so far: 128 weeks · See the bill

The information · Sensitive drawings

I show how the pumps for 40,000 homes are controlled. Not everyone should be able to see me. Somebody has to decide who can.

The wrong way

Friday night…
Working from home

A site engineer wants to work from home, so he emails the control room drawings to his personal account. Nobody marked them as sensitive, so nothing stops him.

A second opinion

On Monday he forwards them to a mate at another firm, for "a second opinion".

A few weeks later…
The forum

The drawings turn up on a hackers' forum. Two hooded figures, grinning at a laptop.

Nobody tells anyone

The engineer tells his supervisor. The supervisor tells nobody. Nobody tells the client.

Opening day…
Backwards

Client: When were you going to tell us?

The pumps start running backwards. Forty thousand homes have a very unusual morning.

The drawings got out in a day. Nobody said a word. On opening day, someone else was running the pumps.

+6 weeks on the back alley's bill: Sensitive drawings with no gate round them: every sensitive drawing reissued and the controls re-secured.
