Think like Priya

An internal audit is a rehearsal, not a verdict. Ask "show me", not "do you?". If the answer is a person's name, the evidence is in their head, and heads go on leave.

Sam's Heads Up Your next auditor might be the client

InfoTown good practice: Rehearse before anyone outside asks.

On your job: The next audit might be the client's, a framework's or a certification body's. Same rehearsal, same "show me".

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me your last internal audit, and what happened to each finding.
  • How do you know the fixes worked?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Internal audits held on the dates planned.
  • Findings with owners, dates and evidence of closure.
  • A follow-up check before the real audit.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Before the real audit

Internal audit belongs to your management system; ISO 19650 doesn't require one by itself. Certification against ISO 19650 is judged on evidence from real projects, which is why a rehearsal pays.
