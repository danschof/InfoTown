Think like Priya

A record only counts if you can find it, trust it and show it to someone in five years' time. Paper in a cabin is one flood, fire or tidy-up away from never having happened. Is each record tied to the drawing and revision it checked?

Watch out for

These are Quality Records!

Proof of what was checked, by whom, against which drawing, kept where it can be found years later.

Sam's Heads Up Your wet well may not have signal

InfoTown good practice: Record at the work face, digitally.

On your job: The bottom of a wet well is rarely a 5G hotspot. Use an app that works offline and syncs later, or scan the paper the same day. The rule is "in the CDE by tonight", not "never touch paper".

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me the inspection records for this part of the work, and which drawing each one checked.
  • If the site office burned down tonight, what would you lose?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Records in the CDE, each with its drawing, revision, photos, signature and date.
  • Nothing that exists only on paper.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Records in the CDE

Quality records belong to your quality system, not to ISO 19650. What ISO 19650 adds is that a record is information like any other: it belongs in the CDE with a status, and if the client needs it, it's part of what you hand over.

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. You'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima.
