Week one

Priya and Sam agree what the safety file needs, who provides each part, and when. It goes on the parts list.

As it changes

The designers send their remaining risks with their drawings, and every change that alters a risk is flagged as it happens: the deeper well, the heavier pumps, the cable.

Every month

The file grows as the job goes, and Priya checks it every month.

Handover

The operations manager reads it, asks one question, gets an answer, and takes the keys.

The scene

From the shared table to the published shelf

Parcels on the shared table carry S codes: shared, and fit for something. To become published, they make one more trip. The firm leading the work authorises the delivery, the client accepts it, and the S becomes an A. If it isn't right, it comes back with comments. And some parcels never make the trip at all. Sam's Heads Up The law got there first

The standard way: ISO 19650 has a part on managing safety information through the job.

On your job: The safety file probably has a legal name and legal contents where you work. Check those first; the standard sits alongside the law, never above it.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me how safety information is gathered during the job, not just at the end.
  • How do design changes reach the safety file?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Safety information on the delivery plan, with owners and dates.
  • The designers' remaining risks issued with their drawings.
  • A file that visibly grows month by month.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Health and safety information

ISO 19650-6 covers health and safety information: gathering it, sharing it and handing it over through the job, like any other information. What the file must contain by law depends on where you work.

Based on the ISO 19650 series: the ideas come from the standard; the plain-English wording, examples and advice are InfoTown's. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. You'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima.
