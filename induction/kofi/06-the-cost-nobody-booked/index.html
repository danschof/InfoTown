Sam's Heads Up Book the cause, not the culprit

InfoTown good practice: Record what went wrong and learn from it.

On your job: If the rework code becomes a way to blame people, people stop using it. Record what went wrong, not who, and use it to fix the route.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • How do you know what poor information is costing this job?
  • What changed because of it?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • A rework cost code, with causes.
  • Monthly figures reported to the leadership team.
  • Changes made as a result, on this job or the next bid.

From the standard
ISO 19650 The cost of poor information

ISO 19650 doesn't measure what poor information costs; that's InfoTown's advice. It does expect lessons learned to be captured and used on future projects.
