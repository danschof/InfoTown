The right way, explained

Every road out of the workshop passes a checkpoint. A guard who waves everyone through because the paperwork looks tidy isn't a checkpoint. He's a gate with a hat on.

If you're the designer

If you're the designer

When you issue information other people calculate from, like weights, loads or schedules, say what changed between revisions. A bigger number buried in C03 will find somebody's old calc.

Sam's Heads Up Your checker may be in another firm

InfoTown good practice: Someone who didn't make it checks it.

On your job: Temporary works may need an independent check from another firm. Same rule: they check the inputs too, and the record says what against.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me how this calculation was checked, and what it was checked against.
  • How does a checker know the inputs were the latest?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Calculations that list their inputs, with sources and revisions.
  • A check record saying what was checked, against what, by whom and when.

From the standard
ISO 19650 Checking before sharing

ISO 19650 expects information to be checked before it's shared with anyone else. How to check an engineering calculation is your own quality process, not ISO 19650's.
