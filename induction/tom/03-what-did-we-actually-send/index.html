The right way, explained

The right way, explained

When the town signs a contract, the signed copy goes to the records office. Not the drafts, not "the one Jim had": the one that was signed.

If you're the information manager

If you're the information manager

Give the bid team a place on the CDE for the submission, and save the final copy as a record the day it goes. Then make sure the delivery team can find it.

From the standard
ISO 19650 The tender response

ISO 19650 expects the tender response to feed into what's agreed at appointment. Keeping the exact submission as a record is InfoTown's advice.
