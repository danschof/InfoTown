Helen's journey

Helen is a divisional director. She signs the contracts and the budgets, and her phone only ever rings with bad news. Six short stops, about ten minutes, on what leaders need to know about information, what they're on the hook for, and what to ask.

Start with Stop 1 →

The Town Hall is where the town is run, not where the work is done. It runs on whatever information the rest of the town sends it. Every decision made there is only as good as that information.

Each stop is a short story about a decision Helen gets wrong, then the same moment done properly, then a four-line briefing and one question to take away. It ends with five questions to ask your information manager.

The six stops

  1. 1What do we actually need to know?Board meeting. Helen asks a simple question about her own division, and gets three answers.
  2. 2Cut it to win itBid review for the pumping station. The price is too high, and the cost plan has a line nobody's sure about.
  3. 3What did we sign?Helen signs the pumping station contract. It makes promises about information that nobody reads.
  4. 4Whose job is it?The site builds from an old drawing. Everyone knows whose fault it is. It just isn't theirs.
  5. 5Can I trust this dashboard?Quarterly review. The dashboard looks lovely. Nobody asks where the numbers came from.
  6. 6The auditA government framework is coming up, and it wants a certificate. The audit is in six weeks.
