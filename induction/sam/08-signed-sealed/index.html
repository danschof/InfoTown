From the standard
ISO 19650 What goes into every appointment

ISO 19650 expects the information side of the job to be part of every appointment, from the client's down to each firm in the team, so that everyone has agreed to it.

In each appointmentIn plain words
The information requirementsWhat that firm has to deliver
The standard, methods and proceduresHow they have to produce and share it
The plan and the delivery scheduleWhat's due when, kept up to date under change control
The information protocolThe contract terms about information: who owns it, who can use it, and for what

From the ISO 19650 series. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. InfoTown explains the ideas in its own words, for learning; you'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
