If you're the client

If you're the client

Leave time between award and start for mobilisation, and ask to see the plan. Award on Friday, start on Monday is a plan to fail politely.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Show me the mobilisation plan, and what was tested before go-live.
  • How did you know the team was ready?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • A mobilisation plan with dates and owners.
  • Test records.
  • Training and access records from week one.
From the standard
ISO 19650 The mobilisation plan

ISO 19650 expects the bid to say how the team will get ready: setting up and testing the systems and ways of working before production starts, and the people who'll be trained to use them.

It coversIn plain words
SystemsSet up, connected and tested
Ways of workingThe methods and procedures, tested on something that isn't live
PeopleTrained, with access on day one
TimingDates that fit the programme, with time to test

From the ISO 19650 series. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. InfoTown explains the ideas in its own words, for learning; you'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
