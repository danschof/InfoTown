The right way, explained

The right way, explained

Before a new firm moves into town, someone checks they can drive on its roads. Asking "can you drive?" and accepting a nod isn't a test.

If you're the client

If you're the client

Ask how capability was assessed across the whole team, not just the lead firm. The weakest link sets the pace.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • How did you assess each firm's capability and capacity?
  • What did you do about the gaps you found?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • An assessment for each firm, with evidence behind it.
  • One summary for the whole team.
  • Actions on the gaps, with training records.
From the standard
ISO 19650 Capability and capacity

ISO 19650 expects every firm in the delivery team to be assessed, then the results put together into one picture of the whole team.

For each firmIn plain words
CapabilityCan they do it: the skills, the know-how, the systems?
CapacityDo they have enough people and time to do it on this job?
The team summaryOne view across every firm, with the gaps showing
The gapsWhat will be done about each one, by whom and when

From the ISO 19650 series. ISO 19650 is © ISO and BSI. InfoTown explains the ideas in its own words, for learning; you'll hear the same language across the industry. Read more for free in the Information Management Initiative (IMI) Framework guidance from BSI and nima. For the full requirements, use the standard itself.
