Every building in town needs someone with the keys. "The council" is not someone. A name and a phone number is.

If you're the client

If you're the client

Ask for named people and how much of their time they'll give, not an org chart full of boxes. Then check they turn up.

For the audit What the auditor will ask
  • Who is responsible for information on this job, and how do you know they're competent?
  • Where is anything you've handed to someone else written down?
For the audit What good evidence looks like
  • Named roles in the plan, with time allocated.
  • Training or competence records for each person.
  • A written record of anything handed to another firm or team.
From the standard
ISO 19650 Who does the information management

ISO 19650 expects the lead firm to say who will carry out its information management, and to name them. Tasks can be given to someone else, like a specialist or another part of the business, but the responsibility stays with the firm that signed.
