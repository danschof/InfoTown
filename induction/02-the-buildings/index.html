The right way, explained

The right way, explained

The buildings in the town are your systems. Some belong to one team. One is shared by everyone and trusted as the record: the Records Office, which on a project is the CDE. Work saved on a desktop or in an inbox never reaches a building at all. It's stuck in someone's bag, and Rex's calc only became safe when it left his.

Fix it Still in your bag? Deliver it.

Still in your bag?
Deliver it.

  1. Move your unfinished work into your team's area on the CDE.
  2. Delete the copy on your laptop so there's only one.
  3. Bookmark the right building so it's the quickest one to reach.

In the town See it in the town: the buildings
The scene

Every district has its buildings

Each district runs on its own buildings: the systems that part of the business works in. Asset Management has the Asset System, Commercial has the Commercial System, and so on. One building, the Records Office, sits in Information Governance and serves every district. Every building keeps information, but each one does a different job. Some are built to last. Some are built for one project and taken down at the end. And some information never reaches a building at all: it stays in someone's bag.

The reveal

These are your Systems, and one of them is your CDE!

The Records Office is the CDE: just another building, but the agreed one. Its storage rooms are controlled, people come to get what they need, and outside parties can meet you there. The other buildings are the systems around it.

ISO 19650 describes the CDE as the agreed source of information for a project or asset, used to collect, manage and share every piece of information through a managed process. The other buildings still matter, but the CDE is the one everybody agrees to trust.
