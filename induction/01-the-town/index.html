The right way, explained

The right way, explained

Every organisation has a hidden town: the digital world where its work really happens. Each part of the business is a district, and each district has its own buildings, the systems where people make, save and find information. Rex's manager handed him a map of that town on day one. Most organisations have never drawn one.

The scene

Your hidden town

Behind the offices and desks you can see, your organisation has a hidden town: the digital world where its work really happens. Each district is a part of the business, a function or department like Pre-Construction or Operations. Each has its own buildings: the systems where its people create, save and find their information. The town was here before your current project began, and it'll still be here when the project ends. Tap a district on the map to look inside.

Watch out for

Looking after this town is Information Management!

Information management means planning, building and running the town, so the right information reaches the right people at the right time.

Standards such as ISO 19650 are guidebooks for running parts of the town on construction and infrastructure projects. InfoTown shows you the whole town first, so the rules make sense when you see them all together in Lesson 12.

Fix it Lost in town? Start a map.

Lost in town?
Start a map.

  1. List the places where you keep and find information.
  2. Draw lines for how it moves between them.
  3. Write who looks after each place.

A note for whoever looks after the town (that's IM): make sure you draw out the town before you try to fix it! You can't improve routes nobody can see...

In the UK each part is published as BS EN ISO 19650, and Part 2 comes with a UK National Annex (the naming and status codes you'll meet in Lessons 7 to 11).
