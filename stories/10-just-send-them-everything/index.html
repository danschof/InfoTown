The real town

Why does handover go wrong?

Because it happens at the end, when everyone's tired and the money's nearly spent.

Nobody planned it

The recipe said what to make, not what to hand over.

Shared feels like finished

S codes get mistaken for A codes all the time.

More feels safer

So everything gets sent, and nothing gets found.

The client's checks are a surprise

Nobody asked what they'd be checking against.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): plan the handover in the recipe on day one: what the client gets, when, who authorises it and how the client will accept it. Handover is a journey, not a dump truck!

Handover
coming up?

  1. List what the recipe says the client gets. Only that.
  2. Check each parcel's S code, then get the delivery authorised.
  3. Agree with the client how they'll accept it, and write it down.
