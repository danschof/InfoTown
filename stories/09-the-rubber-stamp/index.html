The real town

Why do checkpoints get skipped?

Because the deadline was yesterday and the Checker is a nice person.

Deadlines win

Checking feels slow, until you count the rework.

Nobody wrote the rules down

So every Checker checks something different.

Signing is easy

A signature takes two seconds. A check takes longer. Guess which one happens.

It's someone else's job

The maker thinks the Checker will catch it. The Checker thinks the maker got it right.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): write the rulebook before work starts, and build the checkpoints into the route so parcels can't skip them. A stamp without a check is just ink!

Got a rubber
stamp problem?

  1. Pick one recent drawing and ask who actually checked it, and against what.
  2. Write down your four checkpoints and who owns each one.
  3. Put them in the BEP and the CDE workflow, so the route enforces them.
