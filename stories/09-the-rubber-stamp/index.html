Signed, not checked

Friday, 4:55pm

Alex: Can you just sign this off? The client wants it tonight.

To the Checker.

Friday, 4:56pm

The Checker signs it without opening it. Checked in under a minute. A personal best.

Friday, 4:57pm

The approver: Did anyone review it? … It was checked, wasn't it?

Approved anyway, shared, and on its way to site.

Monday

Rex: "But it says CHECKED."
