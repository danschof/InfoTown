Signed, not checked

Friday, 4:55pm

Can you just sign this off? The client wants it tonight.

Alex, to the Checker.

Friday, 4:56pm

The Checker signs it without opening it. Checked in under a minute. A personal best.

Friday, 4:57pm

Did anyone review it? … It was checked, wasn't it?

Approved, shared, and on its way to site.

Monday

The drain goes straight through the foundation.

Dave. Rex: "But it says CHECKED."

The rest of the week

A redesign, a new pipe and a digger standing still for a week.

The result

Three signatures on the drawing. Nobody actually looked. Bloody hell.

Same drawing, actually checked

Friday, 4:55pm

I'll check it properly on Monday. The rulebook gives me a day.

The client gets it Tuesday. Nobody dies.

Monday

Wrong template, and the drain clips the foundation.

Back to Alex with comments. Fixed by lunch.

Tuesday

Right first time? Who are you people?

Dave, genuinely confused.
