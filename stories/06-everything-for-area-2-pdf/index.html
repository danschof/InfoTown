Everything for Area 2.pdf

Drawing 042 is finished, along with 39 of its friends. Alex has an idea to save everyone some clicks.

Before you start

So far

In Lesson 6 you saw that the work travels as parcels: one drawing, one parcel. Alex has forty drawings and a shortcut in mind.

You'll see

  • Why forty drawings in one file is one parcel nobody can control
  • What happens when one drawing in the bundle changes
  • The same Friday, sent as forty parcels

One giant parcel

Friday

Alex: Forty drawings? I'll just put them all in one PDF. Easy.

Saving everyone a few clicks. In theory.

Monday

Dave: Where's the drainage layout? Page 17? Page 23? Page… oh, come on.

Scrolling on a tablet with cold fingers.

Wednesday

One pipe moves on Drawing 042. So a whole new 40-page parcel goes out: "Everything for Area 2 v2.pdf".

Wednesday

Dave: So what's changed?

Nobody can say. He plays spot the difference across 40 pages, and loses.

A month later

The Clerk of Works: Can I just have Drawing 042, the approved one?

It only exists as page 17 of something else.

The result

You could put 40 drawings in one PDF… but you'd lose control of every single one of them.

Same Friday, forty parcels

Friday

Alex shares 40 separate parcels through the CDE, each with its own label, travelling together as one shipment.

Wednesday

Alex: Just 042 has changed. Here it is, revision C02.

One parcel changes, one parcel goes out.

Wednesday

Dave: Got it. One parcel, one change. Back to digging.

He knows exactly what moved, and so does everybody else.

One giant parcelForty drawings squashed together, and nobody knows what changed.
Forty parcels, one shipmentChange one, send one. Everyone can find, check and use each drawing on its own.
