One giant parcel

Friday

Saving everyone a few clicks. In theory.

Monday

Dave: Where's the drainage layout? Page 17? Page 23? Page… oh, come on.

Scrolling on a tablet with cold fingers.

Wednesday

One pipe moves on Drawing 042. So a whole new 40-page parcel goes out: "Everything for Area 2 v2.pdf".

Wednesday

Dave: So what's changed?

Nobody can say. He plays spot the difference across 40 pages, and loses.

The Clerk of Works: Can I just have Drawing 042, the approved one?

You could put 40 drawings in one PDF… but you'd lose control of every single one of them.
