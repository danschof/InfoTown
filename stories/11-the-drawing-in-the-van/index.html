The copy that was handy

Monday, 7am

Dave: Got Drawing 042 right here.

It's been in the van door since March.

Monday, 9am

The trench goes in, exactly where Dave's drawing says.

Monday, 2pm

Rex: Dave… the app says C04. Yours says C02.

Checking. Slightly too late.

Tuesday

The Clerk of Works: Built to which revision?

Dave: "The right one. Probably."

Wednesday

Fill it in. Dig it again. Two days and a lot of swearing.

The result

And nobody can prove what's actually in the ground. Bloody hell, Dave.

Same trench, found and trusted first

Monday, 7am

Dave: Area 2 drainage… there it is.

On the site app. Thirty seconds, including the tea.

Monday, 9am

Dug once, to revision C04.

Monday, 4pm

The Clerk of Works: Built to C04, with the photo linked. Lovely.

Smiling. Rare.
