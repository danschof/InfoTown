The real town

Why do people use the wrong version?

Because the right one is three clicks away and the wrong one is in their hand.

Paper is quicker

Until it's out of date, which is almost immediately.

Search is rubbish

Because the labels are rubbish. See Lesson 7.

No signal on site

So people download it once and never again.

Nobody asked what users need

We build routes around what we make, not around who has to find it.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): start from the need! Watch how people on site actually look for things, then make the right version the easiest one to grab. If the back alley is quicker, people will use it.

People using
old versions?

  1. Ask three people how they found the last drawing they used.
  2. Fix the slowest step: the search, the labels or the signal.
  3. Retire the van-door copies. Politely.
