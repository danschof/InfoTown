The point cloud on a hard drive

A 400GB laser scan of the old pumping station. Too big for the shared system. Just right for a drawer.

The wrong way

Week three

Stevie: Every bolt, Rex. Every bolt.

Stevie laser-scans the old pumping station the new one has to connect to. 400GB.

Week three

Someone says it's too big for the CDE. It goes on a USB drive, and the drive goes in Stevie's desk drawer.

Design office

Alex needs the old pipe positions for the connection. Stevie's on site and the drawer's locked, so Alex works from a drawing dated 1974.

Month two…
Month two

Someone borrows the drive for another job. It comes back. Mostly.

Month five…
Month five

The connection doesn't fit: the 1974 drawing shows what was designed, not what was built. Stevie opens the drawer. The drive clicks, whirrs and dies.

The rescan

The old station is now wrapped in scaffolding, and half of it can't be seen. Rex: "Every bolt?" Stevie: "Some bolts."

The scan saw every bolt. Then it lived in a drawer.
