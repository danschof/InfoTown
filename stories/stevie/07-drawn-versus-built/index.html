The wrong way

The last month…
The last month

Handover is due and somebody asks for the as-built drawings.

One afternoon

The design drawings get AS BUILT stamped on them. Forty drawings, one afternoon, one stamp.

One afternoon

Rex: "Shouldn't someone check them first?" Everyone is very busy.

Back in month seven

The overflow manhole moved 1.2m to dodge a cable nobody knew about. It was agreed with a photo and a thumbs-up.

Handover

The client accepts the pack. Everyone goes home.

Two years later…
Two years later

The operator's crew digs where the drawing says the manhole is. They find a fence post. The manhole is 1.2m away, under a parked van.

The drawings said "as built". They meant "as drawn".
