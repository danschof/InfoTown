The wrong way

Month one…
Month one

Stevie's team finishes a fresh ground survey of the whole site. He saves it to his laptop, in a folder called SURVEY_NEW.

Design office

Alex asks for a survey. Someone finds one in the client's old archive. It's five years old. Nobody checks the date.

Since then

The client has built a car park and raised the access road. The old survey has never heard of either.

Design

Alex designs the levels from it: the inlet, the access ramp, the drainage falls. Beautifully.

Month four…
On site

Rex: Is that meant to be a step?

The new ramp meets the road 400mm below it.

Redesign

Levels, drainage and kerbs, all redesigned. Alex: "We used the survey we had." Stevie looks at his laptop. "Aye. That one's on me."

The right survey existed. It was on Stevie's laptop.
