Only on Rex's laptop

Monday

Rex: I'll save it on my desktop and tidy it up later.

He will not tidy it up later.

Tuesday

To share it, Rex emails a copy to Alex and drops another into the team chat. Generous.

Wednesday

Alex marks up the emailed copy. Rex keeps working on his desktop one. Two versions, and counting.

Thursday

Rex's laptop dies. IT hands him a lovely shiny new one. The desktop? Gone.

Friday

Alex: Which version went to the client? Mine, yours or the one in the chat?

Rex goes very quiet.

The result

Half a day rebuilding the calculation from old emails, and nobody can swear it matches what the client has.

The calc was saved where only Rex could find it.
