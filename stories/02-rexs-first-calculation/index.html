Rex's first calculation

Week two. Rex has found the buildings, but hasn't yet worked out which one to keep things in.

Stuck in Rex's bag

Monday

I'll save it on my desktop and tidy it up later.

Rex. He will not tidy it up later.

Tuesday

To share it, Rex emails a copy to Alex and drops another into the team chat. Generous.

Wednesday

Alex marks up the emailed copy. Rex keeps working on his desktop one. Two versions, and counting.

Thursday

Rex's laptop dies. IT hands him a lovely shiny new one. The desktop? Gone.

Friday

Which version went to the client? Mine, yours or the one in the chat?

Alex. Rex goes very quiet.

The result

Half a day rebuilding the calculation from old emails, and nobody can swear it matches what the client has.

Same week, in the Records Office

Monday

Rex saves the calculation in the team's work-in-progress area on the CDE. Not the desktop. Growth.

Thursday

The laptop still dies (they always do). Rex logs in on a spare and carries on. Nothing lost.

Friday

I've marked up the one on the CDE. There's only one, so we can't get it mixed up.

Alex

In Rex's bagThree copies, one dead laptop and half a day of detective work.
In the Records OfficeOne copy everyone works on, still there when the laptop isn't.
The building test

Is your work in a proper building?

Would it survive if your laptop died?

Proper buildingYes. It lives on the CDE, not on your machine.
In a bagNo. It goes with the laptop.

Can your team find it without asking you?

Proper buildingYes. It's where everyone expects it.
In a bagOnly if you're in, and you remember.

Does someone look after the building?

Proper buildingYes. It's backed up, managed and has a keeper.
In a bagJust you, and only when you remember.
The real town

Why does information stay in people's bags?

Because it's right there, and it never asks any questions.

It's one click away

The desktop is always closest, and it never asks you to fill anything in.

The proper building feels slow

Log in, find the folder, fill in six fields. Sigh.

Nobody said which building

New starters copy whatever the person next to them does. Bad habits included.

No signal on site

No signal in the cabin means saving to the laptop and hoping for the best.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): if more parcels are sitting in people's bags than in the Records Office, don't blame the people. Fix the road to the Records Office!

Still in your bag?
Deliver it.

  1. Move your work in progress into your team's area on the CDE.
  2. Delete the copy on your laptop so there's only one.
  3. Bookmark the right building so it's the quickest one to reach.
