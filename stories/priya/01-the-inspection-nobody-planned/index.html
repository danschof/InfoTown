The wrong way

Thursday

The wet well's base slab. Reinforcement fixed on Thursday. Concrete booked for Friday at 7am, because concrete is always booked for Friday at 7am.

Page 30

The client's inspection plan says the Clerk of Works inspects the reinforcement before any pour. It's on page 30. Nobody on site has met page 30.

Friday, 6.55am…
Friday, 6.55am

Six trucks queue at the gate, and Rex waves them in. Priya rings Dave: "Has the steel been inspected?" Dave: "It looked fine. Pour it."

Monday…
Monday

Clerk of Works: Show me the reinforcement inspection.

The Clerk of Works. Not smiling.

Monday

There isn't one. There are four photos on Dave's phone, three of them of his thumb.

Three weeks

Cores are drilled through the new slab to prove the steel is there. Three weeks lost. Priya, quietly: "If it isn't written down, it didn't happen." Clerk of Works: "Exactly."

The steel was probably fine. Nobody could prove it.
