The wrong way

Week one

The method statement for digging the wet well, C01 (the first version issued for use), is briefed to the gang and pinned to the cabin wall. Rex signs the briefing sheet.

Week three…
Week three

The ground is wetter than the boreholes said. The temporary works designer changes the shoring, and the method statement is reissued as C02. It's emailed to the site manager, who's on leave.

Monday…
Monday

A new gang starts on the wet well. The supervisor briefs them from the copy on the wall: C01.

Monday

They dig the next stage the old way, with the old shoring.

Mid-afternoon

One side of the dig slumps. Nobody is in it. It's close.

The investigation

Work stops for an investigation. Priya: "Which revision were they briefed on?" The briefing sheet doesn't say. It never has.

C02 existed. The gang was briefed on C01.
