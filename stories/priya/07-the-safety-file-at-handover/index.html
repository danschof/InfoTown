The wrong way

The week before handover…
The week before handover

"Who's doing the health and safety file?" Silence. Rex is handed an empty folder and a deadline.

Monday

Rex searches every inbox for "safety". He finds 600 emails, three risk assessments and a lunch menu.

Tuesday

He builds the file from the original design documents. They're a year old.

Since then

The wet well has gone 2m deeper, the pumps have got heavier, and a live cable now runs past the overflow chamber. None of that is in the file.

Handover

Operations manager: Where are the risks for the people who'll go down that wet well?

The client's operations manager reads it before taking over.

Handover

The client won't take the pumping station until the file is right. The people who knew the answers have moved to other jobs. Priya, quietly: "It isn't paperwork, Rex. It's what keeps the next person safe."

A year of safety information, rebuilt from inboxes in a week.
