The wrong way

All year

Every signed inspection sheet goes into a red ring binder in the site cabin. Forty of them. Very tidy.

All year

The binder lives on the bottom shelf, next to the kettle. "Everyone knows where it is."

November…
November

The river next door has opinions. The cabin floods to the knee.

The week after

The Clerk of Works asks for the drainage pressure test records. They were in the binder.

The week after

Priya peels a page apart. "Pressure test: pa…" The rest is soup.

Three weeks

Two drainage runs are dug up and tested again, and the client holds back that part of the payment until they are. Three weeks and a lot of mud.

Forty signed records. One flood. None left.
