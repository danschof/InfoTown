Without a map

Monday, 9am

Rex's new manager: Welcome aboard! Here's your laptop. Shout if you need anything.

Already halfway out of the door.

Monday, 11am

Alex: Drawings? Some are on the big system, some are on the shared drive, and Dave keeps the site ones in his van. Probably.

Designer, trying to be helpful.

Wednesday

Rex draws a map on a notepad. By Wednesday lunchtime it looks like a plate of spaghetti.

Thursday

Rex finds a folder called OLD STUFF and uses a calculation template from it. Bloody hell, Rex! It's three years out of date!

A month later

Rex: There's ANOTHER drive?

A month in, and Rex is still discovering new places where information lives. Like an explorer, but sadder.
