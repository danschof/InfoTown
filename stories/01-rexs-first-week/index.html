Rex's first week

Rex has just joined the organisation as an assistant engineer. Nobody has shown Rex the town.

Before you start

So far

In Lesson 1 you found out that every organisation has a hidden town, and that most people have never seen theirs. Now meet someone who's just moved in.

You'll see

  • What a new starter's first week looks like without a map
  • How information ends up in places nobody can find
  • The same week, with the town drawn out first

Rex's diary, before this story. Day 1. New job at a big Tier 1, on a massive project. Got a laptop and a login. Nobody's said where anything lives yet.

Without a map

Monday, 9am

Rex's new manager: Welcome aboard! Here's your laptop. Shout if you need anything.

Already halfway out of the door.

Monday, 11am

Alex: Drawings? Some are on the big system, some are on the shared drive, and Dave keeps the site ones in his van. Probably.

Designer, trying to be helpful.

Wednesday

Rex draws a map on a notepad. By Wednesday lunchtime it looks like a plate of spaghetti.

Thursday

Rex finds a folder called OLD STUFF and uses a calculation template from it. Bloody hell, Rex! It's three years out of date!

A month later

Rex: There's ANOTHER drive?

A month in, and Rex is still discovering new places where information lives. Like an explorer, but sadder.

The result

Every new starter draws this map from scratch. Every map is different. None of them are right.

Same Monday, with a map

Monday, 9am

Rex's new manager: Here's your laptop. And here's the town map: everything we use, and how it all connects.

Who has clearly done this before.

Monday, 11am

Rex needs a drawing. The map says which building it's in. Five minutes, job done, kettle on.

Friday

Rex: Right, here's the map. Let me show you where everything lives.

A week in, showing the next new starter around like he owns the place.

Without a mapA month to learn the town, a template older than the coffee machine, and a different picture in every head.
With a mapOne picture everyone shares. Rex was useful by Monday lunchtime. Mostly.
