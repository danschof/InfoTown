Drawing 042 takes a shortcut

A drainage drawing, a Friday afternoon and a pour booked for Monday.

The back alley

Friday, 4:05pm

Can you just send me the approved drawing? We're pouring Monday morning.

Dave, site manager

Friday, 4:20pm

It's still being checked... I'll just send you a photo from my phone.

Alex, designer. And so the parcel heads down the back alley.

Monday, 7:30am

The gang builds from the photo propped on a cone. Nobody on site knows the pipe level changed during checking. What could possibly go wrong?

Three weeks later

This pipe is 150mm higher than the approved drawing.

The Clerk of Works. Not smiling.

Can you find it later?

Dave searches his email, his chats and a USB stick from the glovebox. The photo was on his old phone, which went back last week. Which version did they build from? Nobody has a clue.

Can you prove it?

Can you show us what you were given, and who gave it to you?

No trail, no proof. The contractor pays to dig it all up and do it again. Ouch.

Same Friday, on the proper route

Friday, 4:20pm

It isn't approved yet. The checker's on it first thing Monday, and it'll be on the CDE by nine.

Dave grumbles, then moves the pour to Tuesday.

Monday, 9:12am

C01 goes through the Site Office, gets booked in and goes out to site. The gang builds to the new pipe level. Boring. Brilliant.

Three weeks later

Here's what we built to, who approved it and when it reached us.

Found and proven. Query closed the same day. Dave buys the teas.

The back alleySaved half a day on Friday. Cost a rework, a dispute and three weeks of grief.
The proper routeCost half a day on Friday. Nothing went wrong after that. Funny, that.
The back alley test

Two questions for every parcel you send or receive

Can you find it later?

Proper routeIt's in the CDE with its label. Anyone with access can find it, even after the sender has left the job.
Back alleyIt's in one person's phone, inbox or chat. When that person moves on, it goes with them.

Can you prove it?

Proper routeThe CDE records who sent which version, when, and what state it was in.
Back alleyThe parcel arrived, but there's no record of what it was, who sent it or whether it was approved.
The real town

Why do people use back alleys?

Usually for a good reason. Back alleys aren't drawn on the official map, but every town has them.

It's quicker

The proper route feels painfully slow when the pour is on Monday.

They don't know the road

Nobody ever showed them how to send it properly.

The road is closed to them

No CDE access, or the folder they need doesn't exist yet.

Everyone else does it

Use an alley often enough and it starts to feel like the road.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): if everyone's using the same back alley, the proper route is probably in the wrong place. Find the busy alleys and fix the route!

Already in the alley?
Bring it back.

  1. Put the parcel on the CDE.
  2. Record the instruction: who sent it, what it was and when.
  3. Tell the team where to find it.
