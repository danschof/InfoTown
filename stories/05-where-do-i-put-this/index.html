A site nobody connected

Monday, 7am

Rex: Morning! Er… where's the site office? And who do I sign in with?

Nobody knows. There's a portaloo and a skip.

Monday, 8am

Dave: The client's sending everything by email. To everyone. Every hour.

Site manager, with 247 unread messages.

Tuesday

Alex: It's all on our system. Can't you just log in?

Rex: "With what?" Nothing connects the two firms' systems.

Wednesday

Three folders called Drawings, in three different places. Everyone picks a different one.

Thursday

Dave: Photos? On my phone. And the gaffer's. And the phone I dropped in the excavation.

Site photos, scattered across three phones.

The result

Two months in, they're still setting up the site, while trying to build on it. Bloody hell.

Day one, and nothing was ready.
