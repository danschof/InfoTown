Where do I put this?

Rex moves onto his first project site. It's day one. Nobody has set anything up.

A site nobody connected

Monday, 7am

Morning! Er… where's the site office? And who do I sign in with?

Rex. Nobody knows. There's a portaloo and a skip.

Monday, 8am

The client's sending everything by email. To everyone. Every hour.

Dave, site manager, with 247 unread messages.

Tuesday

It's all on our system. Can't you just log in?

Alex, from Designer Town. Rex: "With what?" There's no railway between the towns.

Wednesday

Three folders called Drawings, in three different places. Everyone picks a different one.

Thursday

Photos? On my phone. And the gaffer's. And the phone I dropped in the excavation.

Dave. Site evidence, scattered across three bags.

The result

Two months in, they're still setting up the site, while trying to build on it. Bloody hell.

Same project, set up before day one

Two weeks before

The Coordinator builds the Site Office, connects every town, tests each line and puts up the signposts.

Day one, 7am

Rex signs in at the gate, gets his access, and follows the signposts.

Day one, lunchtime

Drawings in the Site Office, photos in the app, RFIs in the register. Sorted. Where's the kettle?

Rex, knowing where everything goes by lunch.

Nobody set it upEmail avalanches, three "Drawings" folders, and two months of setting up while building.
Built before day oneOne Site Office, every town connected, signposts up. Rex was useful by lunchtime.
The site test

On day one, can everyone answer…

Where does my work go?

SignpostedEvery kind of parcel has one home, and it says so.
Not signposted"Try the shared drive. Or ask Dave."

How does it reach the other towns?

ConnectedA tested line to the designers and to the client.
Not connectedEmail. Lots and lots of email.

Who let me in?

A real gateAccess set up, and taken away again when you leave.
No gateSomeone shared their login. Nobody ever turned it off.
The real town

Why do sites start before they're set up?

Because the diggers are booked, and setting up feels like it can wait.

Everyone's in a rush

The start date was fixed months ago. The site office wasn't.

Every job is set up differently

So nobody knows what "ready" looks like.

Connections are "an IT thing"

Until the designers can't get their drawings in.

Nobody tests it

The first test is day one, with everyone watching.

A note for the Coordinators (that's IM): build and test the Site Office before day one! A site you set up while you're building on it is a site nobody trusts.

Already on site,
and it's a mess?
