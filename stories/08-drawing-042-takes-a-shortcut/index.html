Drawing 042 takes a shortcut

A drainage drawing, a Friday afternoon and a pour booked for Monday.

Rex's diary

Rex's diary, before this story. Week 12. Friday, 4pm. Dave needs a drawing for Monday. The proper one isn't published yet.

The quick way

Friday, 4:05pm

Dave: Can you just send me the drawing for construction? We're pouring Monday morning.

Friday, 4:20pm

Alex: It's still being checked... I'll just send you a photo from my phone.

And so the drawing goes the quick way.

Monday, 7:30am

The gang builds from the photo propped on a cone. Nobody on site knows the pipe level changed during checking. What could possibly go wrong?

Three weeks later

The Clerk of Works: This pipe is 150mm higher than the drawing issued for construction.

Not smiling.

Can you find it later?

Dave and Rex search his email, his chats and a USB stick from the glovebox. The photo was on his old phone, which went back last week. Which version did they build from? Nobody has a clue.

Can you prove it?

The Clerk of Works: Can you show us what you were given, and who gave it to you?

No record, no proof. Kofi does the sums: the contractor pays to dig it all up and do it again. Ouch.

Dave got a drawing. Nobody can prove which one.
