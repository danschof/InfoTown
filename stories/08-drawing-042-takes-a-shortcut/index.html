The back alley

Friday, 4:05pm

Dave: Can you just send me the approved drawing? We're pouring Monday morning.

Friday, 4:20pm

Alex: It's still being checked... I'll just send you a photo from my phone.

And so the parcel heads down the back alley.

Monday, 7:30am

The gang builds from the photo propped on a cone. Nobody on site knows the pipe level changed during checking. What could possibly go wrong?

Three weeks later

The Clerk of Works: This pipe is 150mm higher than the approved drawing.

Not smiling.

Can you find it later?

Dave searches his email, his chats and a USB stick from the glovebox. The photo was on his old phone, which went back last week. Which version did they build from? Nobody has a clue.

Can you prove it?

The Clerk of Works: Can you show us what you were given, and who gave it to you?

No trail, no proof. The contractor pays to dig it all up and do it again. Ouch.

That was the back alley.
